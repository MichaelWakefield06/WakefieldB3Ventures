Welcome aboard

A cozy little harbor in the heart of Chetek.

Hope & Anchor is a locally loved coffeehouse and cafe on 2nd Street, just a short walk from the water. Grab a coffee before you head out on the lake, or settle into a cozy corner and stay a while. There's a spot for every mood, quiet nooks, comfortable tables, a sunny patio, and a drive-thru for the mornings you're in a hurry.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where are you located?

We're at 526 2nd Street, Suite 2, in downtown Chetek, WI, just across from the bait shop and a short walk from the water.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 7am to 3pm, and Saturday from 8am to 3pm. We're closed on Sundays.

What do you serve?

Hot, iced, and blended coffee and espresso, teas, smoothies, and lemonades, plus a full cafe for breakfast and lunch, crepes, paninis, bagels, and handmade chocolates.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. You can order online for pickup, and we have a drive-thru for when you're on the go.

Do you have seating?

We do, indoor seating with cozy corners and comfortable tables, plus a sunny patio when the weather's right.
